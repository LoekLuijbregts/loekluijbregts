Not a restart, a broader destination

This third business is the clearest form of what I have done since 2004.

In 2004, I built my first webshop and learned how to help strangers understand, trust and act through online communication, positioning and what we now call customer-created content or UGC. I later spent several years advising entrepreneurs and commercial teams in the B2B agency world.

Over the past six years, I built GravelBoost and later Cycling Incubators B.V. as an entrepreneur. Not because those businesses failed, but because I grew and discovered that my most valuable contribution applies far beyond the cycling world.

I genuinely love the moment an entrepreneur regains trust in what has already been built and sees more clearly what it can mean for ideal customers. That is where I belong: not as an applause machine, but as a partner who connects patterns, names what he sees and helps make that value visible, sellable and protected.
