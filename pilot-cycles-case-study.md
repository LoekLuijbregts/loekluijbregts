# Pilot Cycles Case Study: 9.48 Million Views and 255 Email Leads for €8,000+ Bikes

**By Loek Luijbregts - Cycling Incubators B.V.**  
[loekluijbregts.com](https://loekluijbregts.com) · [cyclingincubators.com](https://cyclingincubators.com)

Updated 10 October 2026 with the source data.

---

## The Client

Pilot Cycles is a Dutch manufacturer of custom titanium bicycles, founded by Pieter and Joris and based in Nuenen, near Eindhoven in the Netherlands. They build bikes to order: each frame is specced for the rider's measurements, riding style and component preferences. Their bikes use premium components: Pinion Smart.Shift gearboxes, DT Swiss forks, Vittoria tyres.

Pilot Cycles makes a strong product for a specific kind of rider: someone who has ridden enough bikes to know what they want, has the budget to commission something built for them, and understands the difference between a titanium frame built to last decades and a carbon frame on a three-year replacement cycle.

The marketing challenge was not the product. It was reach. Pilot Cycles worked mainly through word of mouth and a regional reputation around Eindhoven. Riders across Europe who might want what Pilot builds were hard to reach through the channels Pilot used.

---

## The Engagement

From February to the end of March 2026, Pilot Cycles and I worked together. The goal was qualified attention from riders who fit a custom titanium bike, at a moment when they were already engaged.

The engagement was built around two content moments that I chose and financed myself. Pilot Cycles did not organise or fund them. Both were independent stories that featured Pilot Cycles bikes because the bikes genuinely suited the challenge, not because a brief had been written around them.

Content made for a brand brief serves the brand. Content that records a real experience, where the product appears because it was really there, serves the audience. And content that serves the audience travels further.

---

## Content Moment One: Morocco

A five-day gravel adventure in Morocco, ridden on titanium Pilot Cycles bikes with Pinion Smart.Shift gearing, a DT Swiss fork and Vittoria tyres. The route crossed mountain passes, gravel tracks and remote landscapes that tested rider and equipment in conditions rarely met in Europe.

---

## Content Moment Two: CyclingWorld Düsseldorf

CyclingWorld Düsseldorf is an annual cycling show in Germany. In 2026 it ran from 19 to 21 March. Pilot Cycles had not prioritised or budgeted for it; going was my own call.

Two reels posted on 21 March, during the show, reached about 2.38 million views together. The reel on Pilot's channel shows exactly 1,900,936 views in its insights. These views are already included in the total below and are not counted twice.

---

## What the Source Data Shows

**9.48 million views on Pilot's own channels.** Facebook counts 4,419,414 views across 71 posts in the Meta export. Instagram counts at least 5.06 million across public reels, trial reels and photos, from the in-app insights. Together, rounded, 9.48 million, as counted in the July 2026 evaluation. Ambassador content is excluded.

**255 unique email leads.** The scorecard received 270 submissions from 255 different email addresses. The number is deduplicated.

**What these numbers do not say.** Leads are not sales. Sales directly traced to these leads have not been established. A bike of €8,000+ is not an impulse purchase, so the effect needs to be followed over a longer period.

---

## What This Means for Custom Bicycle Marketing

**The audience for custom titanium bikes is spread out, but easy to find online.** The riders who want and can afford such a bike live across Europe and beyond, but follow the same platforms and media.

**The moments you did not plan can deliver the most.** CyclingWorld was not on Pilot's calendar. Yet about a quarter of the 9.48 million views came from two reels posted on that one day.

**Authenticity at the price point.** People who buy a custom titanium bike have seen enough equipment to sense whether content was made to sell or because it really happened. Morocco and CyclingWorld worked because they were real.

---

## Summary

Pilot Cycles, 2026: one engagement, 9.48 million views on Pilot's own channels and 255 unique email leads for €8,000+ bikes. Sales from those leads have not yet been established.
