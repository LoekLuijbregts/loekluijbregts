Use ManyChat when

Recognise this?

  • You post useful content but have no clear route from engagement to lead.
  • You manually send the same resource or link dozens of times.
  • Interested people disappear because the next step takes too long.
  • Your social audience grows while your owned email list does not.

The workflow I recommend

Keep the system smaller than the result.

  1. 01

    Promise one specific result

    Create a Reel or post around a desirable outcome and ask people to comment one memorable keyword.

  2. 02

    Deliver immediately in DM

    ManyChat recognises the trigger and sends the resource, tool page or link while interest is still high.

  3. 03

    Ask one useful question

    Learn what they are trying to solve, or ask permission to send the resource by email. Avoid turning curiosity into an interrogation.

  4. 04

    Route by intent

    Tag the subject, invite high-intent people to a scorecard or call, and give everyone else a relevant follow-up.
