Use PhantomBuster when

Recognise this?

  • Your outreach is generic because the source data has no context.
  • Manual profile research consumes more time than thoughtful outreach.

The workflow I recommend

Keep the system smaller than the result.

  1. 01

    Start with a narrow search

    Define role, company type, market and one visible signal. A smaller first list is easier to inspect and improve.

  2. 02

    Extract and enrich live data

    Use a relevant Phantom to capture public profile, company or engagement information and organise it into a useful working list.

  3. 03

    Keep only leads with a reason

    Filter for genuine fit and a timely trigger: a new role, relevant post, event, community or business change.

  4. 04

    Review before you reach out

    Check the person and write from the real context. Respect platform rules, privacy law and human attention; never turn the list into spam.

Your first useful version

Start before you optimise.

  1. 1

    Write one sentence defining the customer and one defining the signal.

  2. 2

    Run a list of 25—not 2,500—for the first test.

  3. 3

    Manually verify the first ten results and refine the source search.

  4. 4

    Contact only people for whom you can explain ‘why you, why now’.
