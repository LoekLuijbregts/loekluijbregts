The workflow I recommend

Keep the system smaller than the result.

  1. 01

    Promise a useful diagnosis

    Frame the scorecard around a decision the participant already wants to make—not around your product features.

  2. 02

    Ask questions that change the follow-up

    Every question should help score fit, identify the weakest area or reveal timing. Remove questions that only satisfy curiosity.

  3. 03

    Return a meaningful result

    Reflect their situation back in plain language, show what is already strong and make the next improvement feel possible.

  4. 04

    Create three paths

    Invite hot leads to a real conversation, nurture warm leads around their weakest score and politely release poor-fit leads.

Your first useful version

Start before you optimise.
