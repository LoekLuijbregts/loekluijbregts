Use Vidonary when

Recognise this?

  • Video has lived on your to-do list for months.
  • You know your subject, but ramble or freeze when the camera starts.
  • One short video still costs you an afternoon and five different apps.
  • Good prospects understand your value only after a long sales call.

The workflow I recommend

Keep the system smaller than the result.

  1. 01

    Choose one buyer-relevant thought

    Start with a question, misconception, moment or client result your ideal customer already cares about.

  2. 02

    Use structure without losing your voice

    Let Vidonary shape the hook and script, then replace generic language with the phrases you would actually say in a room.

  3. 03

    Record before perfection returns

    Use the teleprompter and framing guidance to get a clear take without memorising a monologue.

  4. 04

    Edit for clarity, then publish

    Remove pauses and detours, add captions, resize and publish while the message still feels alive.

Your first useful version

Start before you optimise.

  1. 1

    Write down three subjects clients repeatedly ask you about.

  2. 2

    Record one 45–75 second answer for each subject.

  3. 3

    Publish all three within seven days—do not judge the system on one post.

  4. 4

    Track replies, profile visits and conversations, not just views.
