Use zcal when

Recognise this?

  • Your scheduling page looks unrelated to the experience you sell.

  • Time-zone errors, reminders and calendar admin steal attention.
  • You need separate booking routes for prospects, clients and collaborators.

The workflow I recommend

Keep the system smaller than the result.

  1. 01

    Name the outcome, not just the duration

    ‘30-minute introduction’ is clearer when the page also says what the person should understand or decide afterwards.

  2. 02

    Show who they are meeting

    Use a real image, a short welcome and familiar brand cues so the booking moment feels continuous with the page or message before it.

  3. 03

    Ask only what improves the call

    Two thoughtful questions beat a miniature intake form. Ask what makes useful preparation possible.

  4. 04

    Protect the energy around meetings

    Set buffers, daily limits, reminders and the correct video-call integration before sharing the link.
